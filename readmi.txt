README MONE - Mo ta hoat dong hien tai
=====================================

1. Muc tieu cua MONE
--------------------
MONE la robot social co kha nang tuong tac voi tung nguoi dung theo huong ca nhan hoa dai han.
He thong khong chi tra loi nhu chatbot thong thuong, ma con ket hop nhan dien cam xuc,
tri nho hoi thoai, phong cach giao tiep rieng cua tung nguoi va cac chuc nang giai tri co ban.


2. Luong hoat dong tong quat
----------------------------
Quy trinh chinh cua MONE:

1. Cho goi ten robot bang wake word.
   Vi du:
   - "MONE"
   - "MONE oi"
   - "Aura"
   - "Aura oi"

2. Khi nghe thay ten robot, MONE moi bat dau lang nghe cau noi chinh.

3. MONE chi ghi am khi phat hien co tieng noi.
   Neu nguoi dung im lang hon 3 giay, micro se tu ngat de may nghi.

4. Cau noi dai duoc xu ly bang sliding window.
   Model duoc train voi audio 3 giay, nen cau noi dai hon se duoc cat thanh nhieu cua so 3 giay,
   moi cua so truot 1.5 giay, sau do gop ket qua de suy ra cam xuc cuoi.

5. MONE nhan dien cam xuc tu giong noi.
   Cac nhan hien tai:
   - binhthuong
   - buon
   - gian
   - vui

6. Neu bat camera va co model khuon mat, MONE co the ket hop them cam xuc khuon mat.

7. MONE hop nhat ket qua cam xuc da nguon.
   Neu giong noi va khuon mat mau thuan, he thong co the hoi them cau ngan de xac nhan.

8. MONE bat dau hoi thoai voi nguoi dung dua tren cam xuc da nhan dien.

9. Sau moi phien, MONE luu lich su, tom tat va cap nhat ho so ca nhan hoa.


3. Cac thanh phan chinh
-----------------------

3.1. Wake word

MONE khong nghe va phan tich lien tuc moi am thanh trong moi truong.
Robot chi bat dau xu ly khi nghe nguoi dung goi ten.
Dieu nay giup:

- giam nhieu am nen
- tranh bi kich hoat nham
- tiet kiem tai nguyen
- tao cam giac tuong tac tu nhien hon


3.2. Ghi am den khi im lang

MONE khong cat cau noi theo thoi gian co dinh 3-5 giay.
Thay vao do, robot ghi am den khi nguoi dung noi xong.

Co che:

- bat dau khi co tieng noi vuot nguong am luong
- tiep tuc ghi khi nguoi dung con noi
- dung lai khi im lang qua 3 giay
- neu khong phat hien cau noi that, khong chay AI phan tich


3.3. Sliding window cho cau noi dai

Model cam xuc duoc train voi dau vao co do dai co dinh 3 giay.
Trong thuc te, nguoi dung co the noi cau dai 6 giay, 8 giay hoac hon.

MONE xu ly bang sliding window:

- window = 3.0 giay
- stride = 1.5 giay
- moi window duoc dua vao model rieng
- ket qua duoc lay trung binh xac suat
- nhan co xac suat cao nhat la cam xuc cuoi


3.4. Nhan dien cam xuc da nguon

Hien tai he thong uu tien giong noi.
Neu cau hinh bat khuon mat, MONE co the lay them cam xuc tu camera.

Huong hop nhat:

- voice emotion
- face emotion
- conflict detection
- clarification question neu co mau thuan

Vi du:

Nguoi dung noi nghe co ve binh thuong, nhung mat co ve buon.
MONE se khong ket luan ngay, ma hoi nhe de xac nhan.


3.5. Hoi thoai ca nhan hoa

MONE xay dung prompt dua tren:

- ten nguoi dung
- cam xuc hien tai
- lich su gan day
- tom tat ngay/tuan
- chien luoc tuong tac dai han
- phong cach giao tiep ma nguoi dung thich

Dieu nay giup MONE khong phan hoi giong nhau voi moi nguoi.


3.6. Tri nho hoi thoai

MONE luu lich su theo tung user trong:

soft/user_data/{user_id}/history.jsonl

Moi phien luu:

- thoi gian
- cam xuc giong noi
- cam xuc khuon mat
- cam xuc cuoi cung
- co xung dot hay khong
- noi dung hoi thoai


3.7. Tom tat ngay va tuan

Sau moi phien, MONE cap nhat tom tat:

soft/user_data/{user_id}/summaries/daily_YYYY-MM-DD.txt
soft/user_data/{user_id}/summaries/weekly_YYYY-Wxx.txt

Tom tat gom:

- so phien
- thong ke cam xuc
- mau xung dot
- chu de gan day
- ghi chu ca nhan hoa


3.8. Ho so ca nhan hoa dai han

MONE tao them:

soft/user_data/{user_id}/long_term_profile.json
soft/user_data/{user_id}/interaction_strategy.txt

Noi dung gom:

- tong so phien
- cam xuc chu dao
- thong ke cam xuc tu giong noi
- thong ke cam xuc tu khuon mat
- pattern xung dot voice/face
- phong cach giao tiep nen dung
- phong cach nen giam
- chu de gan day
- chien luoc phan hoi phu hop voi nguoi dung


3.9. Hoc phong cach giao tiep

MONE co the hoi nguoi dung sau mot so phien:

- thich cau tra loi ngan gon
- thich diu nhe, dong cam
- thich MONE hoi nhieu hon
- thich dong vien tich cuc
- thich thang vao van de

Thong tin nay duoc luu trong profile.json va dua vao prompt lan sau.


3.10. Incremental learning

Sau khi nguoi dung xac nhan cam xuc dung/sai, MONE co the luu feature am thanh va nhan da xac nhan.
Khi du so mau, he thong co the cap nhat head cua model cho tung user.

Muc tieu:

- model hieu giong noi cua tung nguoi hon
- khong can train lai toan bo model nang
- co the ca nhan hoa tren thiet bi


3.11. Phat nhac va ke chuyen cuoi

MONE co them nhanh giai tri:

- "MONE oi, ke chuyen cuoi"
- "MONE oi, phat nhac"
- "MONE oi, mo nhac chill"

Neu co file nhac trong soft/music, MONE se mo file local.
Neu khong co, MONE co the mo ket qua tim kiem YouTube.


4. Cac file quan trong
----------------------

soft/run_chatbot.py
- entry point de chay he thong

soft/emotion_chat/main.py
- dieu phoi pipeline chinh

soft/emotion_chat/audio.py
- ghi am, tien xu ly audio, sliding window, du doan cam xuc

soft/emotion_chat/voice_io.py
- wake word, ghi am den khi im lang, speech-to-text, text-to-speech

soft/emotion_chat/chat.py
- vong hoi thoai, LLM/rule reply, phat nhac, ke chuyen cuoi

soft/emotion_chat/fusion.py
- hop nhat cam xuc voice/face

soft/emotion_chat/personalization/history.py
- luu lich su hoi thoai

soft/emotion_chat/personalization/summary.py
- tom tat ngay/tuan

soft/emotion_chat/personalization/profile.py
- ho so ca nhan tung user

soft/emotion_chat/personalization/long_term.py
- chien luoc ca nhan hoa dai han

soft/emotion_chat/personalization/incremental.py
- cap nhat model theo feedback ca nhan


5. Cach chay
------------

Nen chay bang moi truong ao cua project:

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe soft\run_chatbot.py

Neu dung VS Code, chon interpreter:

D:\Aura clam\.venv\Scripts\python.exe


6. Trang thai hien tai cua model .h5
------------------------------------

Model dang dung:

final_production_model.h5

Thong tin:

- input shape: (None, 10, 64, 64, 1)
- output shape: (None, 4)
- label: binhthuong, buon, gian, vui
- validation accuracy gan dung: 95.36%

Hien tai chua can train lai neu chua co them du lieu moi dang ke.


7. Dinh huong thiet ke tong quat
--------------------------------

MONE duoc phat trien theo huong:

Robot social ca nhan hoa dai han dua tren tri nho hoi thoai,
cam xuc da nguon va hoc phong cach giao tiep cua tung nguoi dung.

MONE cang giao tiep lau voi mot nguoi, cang hieu nguoi do can duoc lang nghe,
hoi han, dong vien hay tro chuyen theo cach nao.
