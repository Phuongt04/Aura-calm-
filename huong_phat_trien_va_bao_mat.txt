HUONG PHAT TRIEN TUONG LAI VA BAO MAT DU LIEU CHO MONE
======================================================

1. Dinh huong nghien cuu chinh
------------------------------
Huong phat trien dai han cua MONE:

Robot social ca nhan hoa dai han dua tren tri nho hoi thoai,
cam xuc da nguon va hoc phong cach giao tiep cua tung nguoi dung.

Y tuong cot loi:

MONE khong chi nhan dien cam xuc o tung thoi diem,
ma con hoc dan cach moi nguoi muon duoc lang nghe va tuong tac.

Voi moi nguoi dung rieng, MONE co the hinh thanh:

- tri nho hoi thoai
- xu huong cam xuc
- chu de nguoi dung hay quan tam
- cach nguoi dung phan ung khi buon, vui, gian, met moi
- phong cach giao tiep phu hop
- chien luoc ho tro tinh than theo thoi gian


2. Huong 1 - Ca nhan hoa dai han
--------------------------------

Muc tieu:

MONE cang duoc dung lau, cang hieu tung nguoi hon.

Cac thanh phan co the phat trien:

2.1. Tri nho ngan han

Dung trong mot phien hoi thoai hien tai.
MONE nho nguoi dung vua noi gi, cam xuc hien tai la gi, va chu de dang noi.

2.2. Tri nho trung han

Tong hop theo ngay hoac tuan.
Vi du:

- hom nay nguoi dung noi ve hoc tap
- tuan nay nguoi dung hay met moi
- gan day nguoi dung thich nghe nhac de thu gian

2.3. Tri nho dai han

Tong hop thanh ho so rieng cua tung nguoi.
Vi du:

- nguoi nay thich cau tra loi ngan gon
- khi buon, nguoi nay thich duoc lang nghe hon la nhan loi khuyen
- khi gian, nguoi nay can MONE noi cham va binh tinh
- nguoi nay thuong vui hon khi noi ve gia dinh, am nhac hoac robot


3. Huong 2 - Cam xuc da nguon
-----------------------------

Hien tai MONE co nen tang nhan dien cam xuc tu giong noi va co the mo rong qua khuon mat.
Tuong lai co the phat trien thanh he thong multimodal emotion recognition.

Cac nguon tin hieu:

- giong noi
- khuon mat
- noi dung cau noi
- toc do noi
- do lon am thanh
- khoang lang im
- tan suat tuong tac
- hanh vi nguoi dung lap lai

Vi du:

Nguoi dung noi "toi on" nhung giong noi thap, mat buon va im lang lau.
MONE khong nen ket luan la nguoi dung on that.
Thay vao do, MONE nen hoi nhe:

"MONE nghe ban noi la on, nhung co ve ban hoi met. Ban co muon noi them khong?"


4. Huong 3 - Hoc phong cach giao tiep
-------------------------------------

Muc tieu:

MONE khong dung mot cach noi chuyen cho tat ca moi nguoi.

Moi nguoi co the thich mot cach giao tiep khac nhau:

- ngan gon
- diu nhe
- dong cam
- hai huoc
- hoi nhieu hon
- it hoi hon
- thang vao van de
- dong vien tich cuc
- chi lang nghe

Tuong lai co the nghien cuu:

- hoc tu feedback truc tiep cua nguoi dung
- hoc tu viec nguoi dung co tiep tuc hoi thoai hay khong
- hoc tu cam xuc sau moi cau tra loi cua MONE
- hoc style theo tung boi canh cam xuc

Vi du:

Voi nguoi A:
Khi buon, nen noi diu nhe va hoi it.

Voi nguoi B:
Khi buon, nen dong vien manh va goi y hanh dong cu the.


5. Huong 4 - Chu dong ho tro nguoi dung
---------------------------------------

Hien tai MONE chu yeu phan hoi khi duoc goi.
Tuong lai co the them muc do chu dong co kiem soat.

Vi du:

- neu nguoi dung nhieu ngay lien tuc co cam xuc buon, MONE hoi tham nhe
- neu nguoi dung hay stress vao buoi toi, MONE co the de xuat nghe nhac
- neu nguoi dung lau khong tuong tac, MONE co the chao hoi ngan
- neu nguoi dung thich ke chuyen cuoi, MONE co the de xuat mot cau vui

Can luu y:

Robot chu dong nhung khong duoc lam phien.
Nguoi dung phai co quyen tat tinh nang nay.


6. Huong 5 - Robot vat ly va hanh vi bieu cam
---------------------------------------------

Neu MONE la robot vat ly, nen phat trien them embodiment.

Cac huong mo rong:

- den LED theo cam xuc
- mat hien thi bieu cam
- servo quay dau ve phia nguoi noi
- gat dau khi lang nghe
- chuyen dong cham khi nguoi dung buon
- hieu ung anh sang khi phat nhac
- trang thai "dang lang nghe", "dang nghi", "dang suy nghi"

Dieu nay giup MONE khong chi la chatbot co micro,
ma tro thanh robot social co cam giac hien dien.


7. Huong 6 - Danh gia hieu qua ca nhan hoa
------------------------------------------

Can co thuc nghiem de chung minh MONE tot hon theo thoi gian.

Co the so sanh:

1. MONE khong ca nhan hoa
2. MONE co lich su hoi thoai
3. MONE co lich su + cam xuc + phong cach giao tiep

Chi so danh gia:

- nguoi dung co cam thay MONE hieu minh hon khong
- muc do hai long sau hoi thoai
- so lan nguoi dung tiep tuc tuong tac
- so lan nguoi dung sua nhan cam xuc
- so lan MONE goi y dung nhu cau
- cam xuc nguoi dung co cai thien sau hoi thoai khong


8. Vi sao bao mat du lieu la van de lon
---------------------------------------

MONE la robot social, nen du lieu khong chi la du lieu ky thuat.
No co the gom:

- lich su tro chuyen
- cam xuc ca nhan
- giong noi
- khuon mat
- thoi quen sinh hoat
- chu de rieng tu
- phong cach tam ly cua tung nguoi

Neu dua het len cloud, rui ro se cao.

Cac rui ro:

- ro ri lich su tro chuyen
- lo thong tin cam xuc, tam ly
- nguoi dung mat niem tin
- du lieu bi dung sai muc dich
- server bi tan cong
- API key bi lo
- kho xoa du lieu da backup tren cloud


9. Dinh huong bao mat: Local-first
----------------------------------

Kien truc nen chon cho MONE:

Local-first personalization.

Nghia la:

Du lieu ca nhan hoa duoc luu tren thiet bi/robot truoc.
Cloud chi la tuy chon, khong phai mac dinh.

Du lieu nen giu local:

- raw audio
- anh khuon mat
- history.jsonl
- daily summary
- weekly summary
- long_term_profile.json
- interaction_strategy.txt
- head_weights.h5 cua tung user

Cloud neu co chi nen dung cho:

- cap nhat model base
- backup da ma hoa
- thong ke an danh
- dong bo tuy chon khi nguoi dung cho phep


10. Phan loai du lieu theo muc nhay cam
---------------------------------------

10.1. Muc rat nhay cam - khong nen dua cloud mac dinh

- file am thanh goc
- anh/video khuon mat
- lich su tro chuyen day du
- tom tat ngay/tuan
- ho so cam xuc ca nhan
- ten that, ID that cua nguoi dung

10.2. Muc trung gian - chi gui khi da an danh

- so lan vui/buon/gian/binhthuong
- do chinh xac model
- thong ke loi he thong
- feature da xu ly, khong kem danh tinh

10.3. Muc it nhay cam - co the dung cloud

- version phan mem
- model base
- cau hinh chung
- log ky thuat khong chua noi dung nguoi dung


11. Nguyen tac neu dung cloud
-----------------------------

Neu sau nay muon dua MONE len cloud, nen co cac nguyen tac:

1. Opt-in
Nguoi dung phai chu dong bat dong bo cloud.

2. Encrypt before upload
Du lieu duoc ma hoa tren thiet bi truoc khi gui len server.

3. Khong upload raw audio/face mac dinh
Am thanh va khuon mat nen xu ly local.

4. User-controlled forgetting
Nguoi dung co the noi:

- "MONE, xoa lich su hom nay"
- "MONE, xoa tri nho cua toi"
- "MONE, tat ghi nho ca nhan"

5. Data minimization
Chi luu cai can thiet cho ca nhan hoa.

6. Retention policy
Quy dinh thoi gian luu:

- log tam: 7 ngay
- summary: 90 ngay
- profile dai han: den khi nguoi dung xoa

7. Tach danh tinh
Khong dung ten that lam khoa chinh.
Nen dung user_id noi bo.


12. Huong nghien cuu bao mat nang cao
-------------------------------------

12.1. Federated learning

Moi robot hoc tren du lieu local cua nguoi dung.
Cloud chi nhan cap nhat mo hinh da an danh.
Cloud khong nhan lich su hoi thoai goc.

Y nghia:

Nhieu robot cung giup model chung tot hon,
nhung cloud khong can doc du lieu rieng cua tung nguoi.

12.2. Differential privacy

Them nhieu co kiem soat vao thong ke/gui update,
de giam kha nang suy nguoc du lieu ca nhan.

12.3. Encrypted backup

Neu nguoi dung muon sao luu cloud,
du lieu se duoc ma hoa bang khoa nam tren thiet bi hoac do nguoi dung giu.

12.4. Local LLM

Neu may du manh, uu tien LLM local thay vi gui prompt len dich vu ngoai.
Dieu nay giup giam ro ri noi dung hoi thoai.


13. Huong trien khai de tai
---------------------------

Ten huong nghien cuu co the viet:

"Long-term personalized social robot with local-first privacy-preserving memory."

Hoac:

"Robot social ca nhan hoa dai han dua tren tri nho hoi thoai,
cam xuc da nguon va chien luoc bao ve du lieu local-first."

Muc tieu de tai:

- xay dung robot co kha nang hieu nguoi dung theo thoi gian
- ca nhan hoa cach phan hoi theo cam xuc va phong cach giao tiep
- bao ve du lieu rieng tu bang kien truc local-first
- chi dung cloud khi co su dong y va da ma hoa/an danh


14. Cau chot dinh huong
-----------------------

MONE khong chi la mot chatbot nhan dien cam xuc.
MONE huong toi tro thanh mot robot social co tri nho dai han,
biet hoc cach lang nghe tung nguoi,
dong thoi ton trong quyen rieng tu va quyen kiem soat du lieu ca nhan cua nguoi dung.
